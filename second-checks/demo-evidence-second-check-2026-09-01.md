## Reliability and limitations

- The University of Florida exhibition pages are strong institutional secondary sources for a public-facing lesson.
- The State Department source is useful but retired. It should not be described as currently maintained guidance.
- The demo is a bounded evidence workflow, not a comprehensive Panama Canal historiography. A production lesson should add scholarship representing Panamanian and Caribbean perspectives.
- The visual is illustrative and must never be cited as historical evidence.
